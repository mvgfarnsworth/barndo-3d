Orbit controls

  • Left-drag: spin · Right-drag / Shift-drag: pan
  • Wheel / pinch: zoom · 2-finger drag: pan

Walk controls

  • Click view to capture mouse (Esc releases); or drag to look
  • WASD / arrows: move · Shift: run · Space: jump
  • Room tabs (top) jump to the center of each room; keys 1–7 rooms, 8 Outside, 0 whole building. Or just walk the stairs.
  • F: fly/no-clip · then E/Q (or PgUp/PgDn) up/down
  • V: switch Orbit/Walk · Touch: left thumb moves, right thumb looks
Units: feet. Eye height 5'6". Metal shell 60'×30', 16' eave, 3/12, peak 19'-9". Dimensions from the 2D plan/elevation scripts (L1 rev 10, L2 rev 6).
